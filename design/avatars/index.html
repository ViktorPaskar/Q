A. Aura, painted by your answers

A soft living gradient. Its colours come from the reactions your answers get (❤️ rose, 💚 green, 🥰 peach, 🥹 sky, SAME lime), its shape from your username. It slowly shifts as you answer, so no two people ever look alike.

B. Rings, one for every month you showed up

Each ring is a month, each dash a day you answered. New users start with one thin lime arc; after a year it is a dense, personal pattern. Your avatar literally is your story.

C. Sticker, your photo as a die-cut sticker

Your selfie cut out like our reaction stickers: white rim, own tilt, lime ring that lights up when you've answered today. Before you add a photo, the sticker shows your aura.
