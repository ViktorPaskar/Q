FAQ

Questions about the question

When can I get Quesly?

We're in private beta. Join the waitlist, and when your turn comes we'll email you a personal invite to the app.

Is it free? What's the catch?

No catch. Answering, reading, reacting and sharing are free. There are no ads in the feed, and we never sell your data. One day we may add optional extras, like more story themes, but the daily question stays free.

Who writes the questions?

We do, by hand. Every question is written and tested by us, so it is easy to answer, works in any language and starts a good conversation.

What if I miss a day?

A streak freeze covers you. You earn one for every 7 days in a row and can hold two. If you have no freeze left, your streak starts again from zero. Your Memories stay.

Why can't I see the feed before answering?

So that every answer is your own. Answering first keeps it honest and makes reading everyone else's much more fun.

Is the World feed safe?

It is built to be. There are no comments and no direct messages from strangers, and reactions are positive only. Report any answer or block anyone in two taps, and we review every report. Want only your people? Answer for Friends.
