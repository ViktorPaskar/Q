FAQ

Questions about the question

When can I download Quesly?

Quesly is in private beta on iPhone. Leave your email and we'll send a TestFlight invite as soon as a spot opens. The App Store launch comes after.

Is it free?

Yes. Answering, reading, reacting and sharing are free.

What if I miss a day?

A streak freeze covers you. You earn one for every 7 days in a row and can hold two. If you have no freeze left, your streak starts again from zero. Your Memories stay.

Who sees my answers?

You choose for each answer: Everyone or Friends. Only you can see your reaction counts and who reacted.

Why can't I see the feed before answering?

So that every answer is your own. Answering first keeps it honest and makes reading everyone else's much more fun.

What about bad content?

Reactions are positive-only by design. You can report any answer or block anyone in two taps, and a person reviews every report.

Android?

iPhone first. Leave your email and we'll let you know when Android is on the way.
