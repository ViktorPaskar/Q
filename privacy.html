Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Quesly is a place to answer one question a day and see how friends and the world answered it. This policy explains what we collect when you use the Quesly app and quesly.app, why we collect it, and the choices you have. In short: we collect only what we need to run Quesly, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account at any time.

What we collect

  • Early-access sign-ups. Your email address, so we can send you a beta invite and launch news.
  • Account details. Username, display name, profile photo, bio and location, if you add them.
  • What you post. Your answers, who can see each one (Everyone or Friends), your reactions, saved answers, follows, reports and blocks.
  • Activity needed for features. Streaks, streak freezes, badges and your Memories calendar.
  • Device data. A push-notification token if you allow notifications, plus basic diagnostics (crash reports, app version, device model, OS version) that help us fix bugs.

We don't access your contacts, photos or location unless you choose a feature that needs them and allow it in iOS. Quesly does not run third-party advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run Quesly: show today's question, unlock the feed, deliver reactions and notifications, and keep your streak.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, enforce our Terms, and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To improve the app, using aggregated statistics such as how many people answered a question.
  • To contact you about your account, the beta, or important changes.

Who can see your content

An answer marked Everyone can be seen by anyone on Quesly. An answer marked Friends can be seen only by people you're connected with. Only you can see the reaction counts on your answers and who reacted. If you share an answer to Instagram, TikTok or anywhere else, that platform's policies apply to what you post there.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to host data, send email and notifications, and collect crash reports. They process data only on our behalf and under contract. We share information with authorities only when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account in Settings, we delete your profile and answers within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer. You can unsubscribe from the early-access list at any time using the link in our emails or by writing to us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict how we process it. To use any of these rights, email hello@quesly.app. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Quesly is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you think a child has given us their data, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the changes are significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions? Write to hello@quesly.app.
